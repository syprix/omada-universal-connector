# Community standards

People contributing to this project are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Keep reports and reviews focused on the work. Share only information needed to reproduce a problem, and redact credentials, personal data and private network details.

If you witness behavior that violates these standards, contact the repository maintainers privately. Do not report security vulnerabilities or exposed credentials in a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors.
